Simulators

Software development concepts like technical debt or team dynamics are abstract and hard to visualise. These two are not lectures: they are small models you can turn the dials of and watch.

  • Technical debt — how shortcuts create compound productivity losses over time: the true cost of a shortcut, the break-even point between clean and debt-driven development, and what the interest rate does to it.
  • Developer meetings — how the kind and the timing of meetings affect a developer's focus and fatigue, and with them how many features a week actually finishes. Paint a calendar and see.

Use ls to see them, or cat technical-debt to read one here.
